Widget A — Divergence Card 360 × 150

Built to your spec, and stripped down to one number a trader can read in a second: how far profitable traders are leaning away from unprofitable traders, plotted every minute. Above the line the winners are leaning bullish; below it, bearish. Dots are divergence events — hover any dot for one plain sentence on what happened and what price did next.

Flip bullish Flip bearish 10 / 15 / 20 pt divergence Profitable 60% / 65% one side Rapid positioning change Fired, not confirmed
How a flag gets graded
1An event fires on the divergence line — a flip, a level, a conviction cross, or a rapid swing.
2We watch the next 15 one-minute candles. If at least 5 of them close on the side the divergence pointed and clear it by 0.04% — roughly 9 NQ points, 2.6 ES points — the flag is confirmed, a solid dot. The move requirement matters: "closed one tick the right way" is satisfied by noise alone and would put every card in the 90s.
3If it doesn't, the dot stays hollow and still shows on the card. We publish the misses. The footer keeps the running score — "7 flags today · 5/6 confirmed" — because a widget that is never wrong is a widget nobody believes.
4Divergence is measured in percentage points of long share: profitable traders 62% long vs. unprofitable traders 42% long is a 20-point divergence. Labelled "pts" everywhere so it can't be misread as a price move.

03

Widget B — Live Signal Card

One verdict per market, not a data dump: which side the profitable traders are on, how long they've held it, what price has done since, and how many candles have broken their way. Cohort bars are the receipts underneath. Built-in dropdown to switch markets; three sizes for any grid slot.

04

Widget C — Market Pulse Strip

The whole futures board at a glance: paired bars per instrument — P for profitable traders, U for unprofitable traders — with a tilt arrow for the profitable side and a Δ divergence badge. Designed to sit directly under the stat tiles — the first thing a trader scans before the next copy session. Hover any cell for detail.

05

Widget D — Intraday Positioning Chart

The deep-dive panel: price candles and cohort positioning on one chart, aligned bar-for-bar. Flip markers only fire when a cross holds for 10+ candles, the shaded zone shows time on the current signal, and the verdict line keeps score — price move and candles-right since the flip. Hover for the crosshair; switch instrument and timeframe.

Why this wins for Tradesyncer

Session-long stickiness

Today the dashboard is where traders review results after the fact. Live market positioning gives them a reason to keep it open during the session — more time in product, more surface for everything else you ship in the rebuild.

Data nobody else can offer

COT is weekly and institutional. Broker sentiment is unsplit retail flow. This is live funded-trader positioning split by who is actually profitable — sourced from real prop order flow. No other copier, journal, or dashboard has it.

Zero build lift

One script tag or iframe per widget, themed to your tokens (light and dark, as previewed here). JSON REST underneath, edge-cached at 10 seconds, CORS-ready. Your team integrates in an afternoon, not a sprint.
