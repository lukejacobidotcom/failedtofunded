01 / THE INITIATIVE

Individual experiences.
Shared questions.

We’re investigating reports that slippage has been used to reduce evaluation pass rates and payouts on Vest Markets.

This initiative began with accounts provided to the organizer by traders and sources described as being at Vest. We are gathering firsthand experiences and supporting records to understand the scope and seek a response.

Each submission is a reported experience, not a verified finding. We’ll distinguish what traders report from what the records establish.

04 / BEFORE YOU SHARE

A few fair
questions.

Who should submit a report?+

Anyone who traded on Vest Markets and believes slippage affected an evaluation, funded account, profit, or payout. Describe what you directly experienced, including details that might explain the fill.

Do I need proof to participate?+

No. Start with what you remember. If you have records, keep the original files. Useful details include the instrument, order type, size, timestamp and time zone, expected price, actual fill, and any response from support.

Will my name or story be published?+

No submissions appear on the public site. The organizer can review your report and contact you about it. Only de-identified aggregate findings are contemplated without separate permission; publishing your name, quote, or records would require your separate agreement.

Does a bad fill prove deliberate manipulation?+

No. A report describes an experience. Establishing the cause requires examining the order, timing, market conditions, and platform records. The investigation will keep allegations and verified findings distinct.

Is this a claim for compensation?+

This is an information-gathering initiative. Submitting a report does not start legal representation or guarantee compensation, a payout, or an account reversal.

Can I withdraw my report?+

Yes. After submitting, save the private management link. You can use it to delete your report and contact information from the active collection. The form does not send an email, so keep that link yourself.
