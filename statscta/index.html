Behaviour spec

Entry points
Two. A quiet outlined button on the page title row for people who came looking, and one module below the charts for people who did not. No interstitial, no modal on load, nothing that blocks the stats.
One tap
The trader is signed in, so name and email are already ours. The panel asks one question and nothing else. Do not render a name or email field here.
The question
Do you already have a futures brokerage account. Four options. It decides invite order and tells us what to build first. Store it against the member, not the session.
Suppression
suppressIfBreachedWithinDays: 30. Both entry points return null for those accounts. This is the single most important rule in the component.
Endpoint
POST { answer } returning { position }. The member is identified by session, so no email is sent from the client. On failure keep the panel open and show the error inline; never claim a place in the queue we did not get.
Confirmation
The queue number is the whole screen. It is the only receipt they get. Below it, three plain lines on what happens next, then the Discord route.
No email
Email is off for this programme, so the confirmation must not promise an inbox. Announcements reach people through the dashboard and Discord, and the copy says exactly that. The Discord role is the notification channel, which makes it the most important control on the confirmation screen.
Colour
Talero blue #0048FF throughout, never the MFFU #3A82F7. The module is a different company on your page and should look like one.
Analytics
stats_talero_view on render, stats_talero_open on either entry point, stats_talero_join on success, each carrying which entry point fired it.
Accessibility
The panel takes focus on open, traps it while open, closes on Escape, and returns focus to the control that opened it. The scrim is not the only way out.
Compliance
Disclosure sits in the panel above the join button, not hidden behind a link. Joining does not open an account and the copy says so. All of it is introducing broker promotional material and needs pre-review.
