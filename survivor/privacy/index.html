PRIVACY & DATA USE · OCTOBER 20, 2026

Your experience.
Your information.

This notice describes the Vest Slippage report collection at Failed to Funded. Reports are reviewed privately and are not posted on the public site.

Who receives your report

Failed to Funded is a MyFundedFutures website. MyFunded Futures, LLC operates this collection through the team organizing the initiative. MyFundedFutures offers competing trading evaluation programs. This initiative is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vest Markets.

For questions, access, corrections, or help withdrawing a report, contact support@myfundedfutures.com with “Survivor report” and your report reference. Please do not send trading passwords or account credentials. You can also read the MyFundedFutures privacy policy.

What we collect

The form requires your email, affected account stage, reported impacts, and consent. A name, approximate month, instrument, description, and indication that you have records are optional. We store the submission time, a report reference, the consent version, and a hash of your private withdrawal token.

No trading login, wallet connection, payment information, or document upload is requested. Leave out account numbers, passwords, wallet secrets, and other people’s personal information.

How your report is used

The initiative team may review reports, contact you about your experience or initiative updates, compare reported patterns, and prepare de-identified aggregate findings. A submission is a reported experience, not a verified finding.

This form’s consent covers this initiative. It does not enroll you in trading offers or unrelated marketing, authorize the sale of your contact information, or permit publishing your name, quotations, or underlying records. Identifiable publication requires separate permission. Participation does not require purchasing a MyFundedFutures account.

Storage and access

The page is hosted on Failed to Funded. Reports are sent over HTTPS to the initiative’s Sites-hosted service and stored in its Cloudflare D1 database. Only the authorized organizer account can export the collection. There is no public endpoint for browsing reports or contact information. Service providers process information to operate the hosting and storage.

This intake page contains no advertising tags or analytics scripts. Hosting and authentication providers may process request metadata for service operation. For abuse prevention, the application stores keyed hashes derived from IP metadata with counters that expire every ten minutes and are removed during later requests. Raw IP addresses are not stored in the report database.

Your control and withdrawal

Save the private management link shown after submission. It lets you delete the report and associated contact information from the active database. Anyone with that link can delete the report, so keep it private. No confirmation email is sent.

Contact the address above if you lose the link or need access or correction assistance. We may need to verify your email before acting on a request. Deletion cannot undo de-identified findings already published. Reports stay in the active collection until withdrawn or removed when no longer needed for this initiative. The organizer must also handle any exported copies consistently with withdrawal requests.
