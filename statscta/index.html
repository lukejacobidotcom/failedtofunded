Ten changes I would make

Ordered by what stops the ship, then by what the card is worth once it does.

Blocker

1. The "on the list" state has no way to happen

talero.com is a different company on a different domain. There is no shared session, so the dashboard has no way of knowing a trader joined. As written the chip can never flip, and a trader who signed up last week keeps being asked to sign up.

Fix: pick one. Either the CTA opens an in-dashboard panel that posts to an MFFU endpoint and MFFU forwards the signup, which keeps the state locally and makes the one tap join possible. Or Talero calls back to MFFU on signup, which is a cross entity data flow and needs its own agreement before anyone builds it. The first is far cheaper and is the only one that can ship in the copy-only phase.

Blocker

2. The NFA ID may be the wrong one

talero.com prints two different NFA IDs for MFB Capital on the same page: 0562645 in the Why Talero section and 0573109 in the footer regulatory block. The handoff says keep the line intact, which would copy one of them onto the dashboard without anyone checking which is right.

Fix: confirm against NFA BASIC before build, not at review. A wrong registration number on a regulated entity's marketing is the one error on this card that is genuinely expensive.

High

3. Nothing suppresses it after a breach

The card renders for every trader, including one who blew an account this morning. Pointing that trader at a real money brokerage is the single worst thing this programme could do, and it will be screenshotted.

Fix: suppressIfBreachedWithinDays: 30 as a rule in the component, not a thing to remember. Same rule belongs on every Talero surface.

High

4. The card never says why anyone would want it

Wordmark, product name, button, legal line. A funded trader reading it learns that a brokerage exists, not what changes if they use one. That is the whole job of the card and it is the one thing missing.

Fix: one line above the CTA. "Your own account, your own capital, your own rules." Factual, no outcome claim, no comparison to the funded product, and it answers the only question the reader actually has. It is the cheapest improvement on this list by a distance.

High

5. The gold left edge puts MFFU branding on another company's card

Section 2.3 says the palette exists so the card "reads as Talero and not as MFFU gold", but the shared .mod class gives every module a 2px gold left edge, Talero included. The one deliberate brand signal in the design rules is working against the one deliberate brand decision in the component.

Fix: border-left-color: rgba(79,163,255,.55) on this card only. It still reads as a new module, in the right company's colour.

Medium

6. "Coming soon" tells people there is nothing to do

The waitlist is open right now. "Coming soon" describes the brokerage accurately and the action inaccurately, and it is the reason to scroll past.

Fix: Waitlist open. Equally true, and it is the difference between an announcement and an invitation.

Medium

7. Two filled buttons in one row

Refer a friend ships a filled Copy link button and Talero ships a filled blue CTA, side by side. The design rules say one action per view and reserve fills for the most useful thing on screen. For most traders that is the referral link, not a brokerage that has not opened.

Fix: Talero takes .opp-cta.neutral until the brokerage actually opens, then earns the fill.

Medium

8. You cannot tell whether the card works

Only talero_waitlist_click fires. With no impression event there is no denominator, so there is no click through rate and no way to compare this surface against the header tab or the homepage rail.

Fix: add talero_card_view on render with the suppression state as a property, so suppressed impressions are counted separately rather than silently missing.

Medium

9. "Talero by MFB Capital" implies separateness without saying it

A trader reading a card inside their MFFU dashboard will reasonably assume it is an MFFU product with a different name. The regulatory line names the entity but never says it is a different company.

Fix: three words. "Talero by MFB Capital, a separate company". It costs nothing and it is the sentence a regulator would look for.

Medium

10. The CTA should not take a funded trader out of their dashboard

The spec says the CTA opens talero.com but does not say how. A same tab navigation drops a trader out of a live session, possibly with positions open.

Fix: target="_blank" rel="noopener", and say so in the aria-label. Applied on the card at the top of this page.

Not compliance approved. Every Talero surface is introducing broker promotional material and needs pre-review, versioning and archiving.
