Behaviour spec

Component
Add one tab to ProductSwitcherBar. No new component. The markup mirrors the existing tabs exactly: an <a class="tab"> holding an icon, then labels with title and subtitle, and the badge nested inside the title span.
Icon
Needs a real asset. Futures ships futures-icon.ac5bdd1e869c.svg at 20×24 and Perps ships perps-icon.f5f8c4664e9b.png at 24×24. Add talero-icon.svg under /images/mffu/static/. The blue square standing in for it here is a placeholder, not artwork.
Label
The word is Talero with a Waitlist badge. Never "Brokerage", never "Open an account". A waitlist is a notification list; an account is a solicitation. That one word decides which compliance category the tab sits in.
Badge
Same badge component, recoloured: background #0048FF with white text. Perps green #34C759 with #02040E text is reserved for live products, and the dark text fails contrast on blue anyway. The word is WAITLIST, never NEW.
Destination
https://www.talero.com/waitlist?partner=MFFU, matching the ?partner=MFFU convention the Perps tab already uses rather than introducing a UTM string. Unlike the other two tabs it is target="_blank" rel="noopener", because it leaves the estate.
Subtitle
Futures brokerage, coming soon, tinted rgb(143,180,255). This mirrors how the Perps tab tints its subtitle rgb(127,199,154) to carry its own brand colour.
Separation
A 1px vertical rule before the tab, plus Talero blue on the icon, badge and subtitle. Futures and Perps are My Funded Futures. Talero is MFB Capital, LLC. The bar must not imply one firm.
Disclosure
Hover and focus reveal MFB Capital, LLC · a separate company under the subtitle, 200ms ease-out, disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. The full CFTC and NFA line lives on the destination page.
Active state
Not needed yet: the tab always navigates away, so it never carries aria-current. If Talero ever gets pages inside the estate, follow the existing pattern and give it a taleroActive class whose inset box shadow is #0048FF, matching how Futures uses #3A82F7 and Perps uses #34C759.
Focus
Unchanged from the component: outline: rgb(52,199,89) solid 2px; outline-offset: -3px. Green is the bar's focus colour for every tab and Talero should not invent its own.
Analytics
data-cta="header-talero". Report clicks against waitlist signups so we can tell whether the bar is doing the work.
Sequencing
This is the highest reach surface we own: every page, every visitor, logged out included. Ship it after talero.com carries commissions, the FCM name and its how it works ladder above the fold. Opening this tap into the page as it stands today wastes the best impression we get.
Not compliance approved. Every Talero surface is introducing broker promotional material and needs pre-review, versioning and archiving before it ships. The Talero card, and what I would change

The page above is the approved dashboard demo with two edits: the red review outlines are gone, and Join the Waitlist is now a plain link to talero.com in a new tab. Everything else is untouched. Below, what I would change about the Talero card before it ships, worst first.

Decided

1. The joined state is gone, on purpose

The handoff said the chip becomes "You're on the list" after signup. Talero is a separate company on a separate domain, so the dashboard has no session to read and no way to know. That state could never have fired.

Done: the CTA is a plain outbound link and the joined state is removed from the markup and the script. Live consequence: the card keeps asking traders who already joined. Acceptable for now, and it is the reason to eventually move the signup into the dashboard.

Blocker

2. The NFA ID may be the wrong one

talero.com prints two different NFA IDs for MFB Capital on the same page: 0562645 in the Why Talero section and 0573109 in the footer regulatory block. The card carries 0562645 because the handoff said keep the line intact.

Fix: confirm against NFA BASIC before build, not at review. A wrong registration number on a regulated entity's marketing is the one error here that is genuinely expensive.

High

3. Nothing suppresses it after a breach

The card renders for every trader, including one who blew an account this morning. Pointing that trader at a real money brokerage is the worst thing this programme could do, and it is the screenshot that will circulate.

Fix: suppressIfBreachedWithinDays: 30 as a rule in the component, not a thing to remember. The same rule belongs on every Talero surface.

High

4. The card never says why anyone would want it

Wordmark, product name, button, legal line. A funded trader reading it learns that a brokerage exists, not what changes if they use one. That is the whole job of the card and it is the one thing missing.

Fix: one line above the CTA. "Your own account, your own capital, your own rules." Factual, no outcome claim, no comparison to the funded product, and it answers the only question the reader has. Cheapest improvement on this list by a distance.

High

5. The gold left edge is MFFU branding on another company's card

Section 2.3 says the palette exists so the card reads as Talero and not as MFFU gold, but the shared .mod class gives every module a 2px gold left edge, Talero included. The design rule is working against the component decision.

Fix: border-left-color: rgba(79,163,255,.55) on this card only. It still reads as a new module, in the right company's colour.

Medium

6. "Coming soon" tells people there is nothing to do

The waitlist is open right now. "Coming soon" describes the brokerage accurately and the action inaccurately, and it is the reason to scroll past.

Fix: Waitlist open. Equally true, and it is the difference between an announcement and an invitation.

Medium

7. Two filled buttons in one row

Refer a friend ships a filled Copy link button and Talero ships a filled blue CTA, side by side. The design rules say one action per view and reserve fills for the most useful thing on screen. For most traders that is the referral link, not a brokerage that has not opened.

Fix: Talero takes .opp-cta.neutral until the brokerage actually opens, then earns the fill.

Medium

8. You cannot tell whether the card works

Only a click event was specced. With no impression event there is no denominator, so no click through rate, and no way to compare this surface against the header tab or the homepage rail.

Fix: add talero_card_view on render, carrying the suppression state, so suppressed impressions are counted rather than silently missing.

Medium

9. "Talero by MFB Capital" implies separateness without saying it

A trader reading a card inside their MFFU dashboard will reasonably assume it is an MFFU product with a different name. The regulatory line names the entity but never says it is a different company.

Fix: three words. "Talero by MFB Capital, a separate company". Costs nothing, and it is the sentence a regulator would look for.

Decided

10. The CTA no longer drops a trader out of their session

The spec said the CTA opens talero.com but not how. A same tab navigation pulls a funded trader out of a live dashboard, possibly with positions open.

Done: target="_blank" rel="noopener", with the aria-label saying it opens in a new tab.

Not compliance approved. Every Talero surface is introducing broker promotional material and needs pre-review, versioning and archiving. The card above carries the handoff copy unchanged apart from the link behaviour, so it can be reviewed as specced.
Surface 4

The waitlist page

Four changes to talero.com/waitlist. Talero's own codebase, so it needs a different route to engineering than the three MFFU surfaces above.

Change 1Move the ladder up Join the waitlist, Wait for your invitation, Open your account, Start trading. All four steps are already written and already on the page, sitting under the form where nobody deciding whether to hand over an email will see them. Move the block above the form, or beside it on desktop.
01Join the waitlist
02Wait for your invitation
03Open your account
04Start trading
Change 2 and 3The form Trading Experience goes. In its place, the one question that decides invite order, plus the platform question that decides what to build first. Submit it and you land on Change 4.

Behaviour spec

Change 1
Move the existing how it works block above the form. No new copy, no new component. On desktop it can sit in the left column beside the form; on mobile it goes directly above it.
Change 2
Remove the Trading Experience select. Beginner through Professional tells us nothing we can act on.
Change 3
Add Which describes you today? with four options, required, and Platform you trade on today as an optional select. The first decides invite order. The second decides which platform integration to build first, which is the single biggest factor in whether anyone actually switches.
Change 4
Build the confirmation screen. Today submitting is the end of the experience. The queue number is the only receipt anyone gets, so it is the whole screen, followed by three plain lines on what happens next and a referral link that moves you up the queue.
No email
Email is off for this programme. The confirmation must not promise an inbox, and the live page currently does: it says "Keep an eye on your inbox for upcoming announcements and launch details" and collects a marketing consent. Either that copy changes or the no email rule does. This spec assumes the copy changes.
Endpoint
POST { firstName, lastName, email, describes, platform } returning { position, referralCode }. On failure keep the form filled and show the error inline. Never show a queue position we did not get back.
Validation
First name, last name, email and the describes question are required. Platform is optional. Errors render inline under the field, not as an alert, and the first invalid field takes focus.
Referral
Queue order only. Referring people moves you up the list and changes nothing else. Do not attach points, discounts or any other value to it, which would turn a notification list into an inducement.
Placeholders
[RATE] and [FCM NAME] stay as placeholders: commissions are not locked and the clearing partner is not nameable publicly yet. Ship the page with those two lines absent rather than with invented numbers.
Compliance
All of it is introducing broker promotional material. No earnings claims, no easy or low risk language, no implied trading outcomes. Joining the waitlist does not open an account and the copy says so above the button.
Fix this first, it is unrelated to everything above. talero.com prints two different NFA IDs for MFB Capital on the same page: 0562645 in the Why Talero section and 0573109 in the footer regulatory block. One of them is wrong on a registered entity's public site.
Not compliance approved. This is Talero's codebase rather than MFFU's, so it needs a different route to engineering than the other pages in this repo.
