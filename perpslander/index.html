Common questions

What traders ask most before they start their first challenge.

What is MyFundedPerps?

MyFundedPerps is a perpetuals-focused prop firm from the MyFunded family, built with the technology and team behind Funding Perpetuals. You pass an evaluation, prove your edge, and trade a funded account with up to $500,000 in simulated capital on a platform from the team behind My Funded Futures.

Is my My Funded Futures account the same account?

No. MyFundedPerps is a separate product with its own account, login, terms and support. Your futures accounts, payouts, loyalty status and affiliate arrangements are unaffected, and you sign up for perpetuals separately.

Is real money at risk?

No. Every MyFundedPerps account, both evaluation and funded, operates in a simulated, non-executing environment. No participant funds a live trading account, and no participant order is routed to a public market or exchange.

Pricing is modeled by reference to publicly available data from third-party venues so that conditions approximate a real book. Balances, fills, margin, liquidations and payouts are simulated within the program.

What can I trade?

The program opens with perpetual contracts on digital assets, where liquidity is deepest. Coverage of metals, energy, foreign exchange, indices and equities is expanding. The markets available to you depend on your model and your region, and the live list is always the one shown inside the platform.

How do payouts work?

Once you are funded you can request a payout every 7 days, with 24-hour guaranteed processing, and you keep up to 90% of simulated profits. Full payout rules are published on the MyFundedPerps site.

Can I run bots or AI agents?

Yes. MyFundedPerps ships a developer platform with REST, WebSocket and MCP access plus SDKs, so you can connect algorithmic strategies, hosted bots and AI agents directly to your account. Prohibited strategies are set out in the program rules.

Who can open an account?

Eligibility depends on your country of residence and on identity verification at signup. Some jurisdictions are restricted. The current restricted-country list and eligibility requirements are published on the MyFundedPerps site and are checked during signup.

Important disclosures

Simulated environment, no real capital, no live trading All accounts offered through MyFundedPerps, including both evaluation accounts and simulated funded accounts, operate exclusively within a simulated, non-executing environment. No program involves the deposit, use or risk of real capital, and no participant is asked to fund a live trading account.

Reference pricing, no affiliation with referenced exchanges Simulated pricing and settlement are modeled by reference to publicly available data from third-party exchanges. MyFundedPerps is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by those venues, and simulated accounts do not connect to or interact with any real account, order book or funds.

Hypothetical performance Hypothetical performance results have inherent limitations. Unlike an actual performance record, simulated results do not represent actual trading, and because the trades have not been executed the results may under-compensate or over-compensate for the impact of market factors such as lack of liquidity.

Separate products My Funded Futures and MyFundedPerps are operated as separate products with separate accounts, logins, terms and support. Purchasing or holding an account with one does not create an account or entitlement with the other.
