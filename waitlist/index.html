How the club works

Three steps. That's the whole loop.

Trade, get paid, invite — all three pay points. Points buy packs. Packs hold rewards you can't buy anywhere else.

1

Earn points

Points land for the things you already do — starting evaluations, passing them, receiving payouts, showing up daily. Nothing is based on trade size, frequency or profit.

2

Open packs

Spend points on a sealed pack and pull your cards. Contents and odds are printed on the front before you commit — and every pack delivers, so there's no empty pull.

3

Redeem rewards

Discount cards, merch and members-only access. Hold them in your inventory and use them when you're ready.

Terms Privacy Disclaimer Help Centre

MyFunded Club membership. Membership is free and no purchase is necessary to enrol. Open to individuals aged 18 or over, one membership per person. Employees, contractors and their immediate families are not eligible. Void where prohibited; residents of Ontario and Quebec are excluded.

Points may be earned without identity verification, but accepted KYC/AML verification is required before any reward can be redeemed. Points carry no cash value, are non-transferable, and are forfeited if an account is suspended or terminated. Pack contents, odds and value ranges are disclosed before points are committed. Reward items are single-use and expire 30 days after issue. Point values, pack contents and reward availability are subject to change during beta. Full programme terms govern and are available at enrolment.

Qualifying activity is never based on trade size, frequency, position risk or profit amount. An invite qualifies only when the invited person is a genuinely new customer who completes a qualifying purchase within 30 days of enrolling. Self-referrals and same-household referrals are void, and points are reversed on refund or chargeback.

Trading futures involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor. Nothing on this page is a solicitation, a recommendation, or a representation that any trading outcome, payout or level of profitability is typical, likely or guaranteed. MyFundedFutures evaluations are simulated-trading assessments.
