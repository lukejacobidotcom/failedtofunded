The Talero card, and what I would change

The page above is the approved dashboard demo with two edits: the red review outlines are gone, and Join the Waitlist is now a plain link to talero.com in a new tab. Everything else is untouched. Below, what I would change about the Talero card before it ships, worst first.

Decided

1. The joined state is gone, on purpose

The handoff said the chip becomes "You're on the list" after signup. Talero is a separate company on a separate domain, so the dashboard has no session to read and no way to know. That state could never have fired.

Done: the CTA is a plain outbound link and the joined state is removed from the markup and the script. Live consequence: the card keeps asking traders who already joined. Acceptable for now, and it is the reason to eventually move the signup into the dashboard.

Blocker

2. The NFA ID may be the wrong one

talero.com prints two different NFA IDs for MFB Capital on the same page: 0562645 in the Why Talero section and 0573109 in the footer regulatory block. The card carries 0562645 because the handoff said keep the line intact.

Fix: confirm against NFA BASIC before build, not at review. A wrong registration number on a regulated entity's marketing is the one error here that is genuinely expensive.

High

3. Nothing suppresses it after a breach

The card renders for every trader, including one who blew an account this morning. Pointing that trader at a real money brokerage is the worst thing this programme could do, and it is the screenshot that will circulate.

Fix: suppressIfBreachedWithinDays: 30 as a rule in the component, not a thing to remember. The same rule belongs on every Talero surface.

High

4. The card never says why anyone would want it

Wordmark, product name, button, legal line. A funded trader reading it learns that a brokerage exists, not what changes if they use one. That is the whole job of the card and it is the one thing missing.

Fix: one line above the CTA. "Your own account, your own capital, your own rules." Factual, no outcome claim, no comparison to the funded product, and it answers the only question the reader has. Cheapest improvement on this list by a distance.

High

5. The gold left edge is MFFU branding on another company's card

Section 2.3 says the palette exists so the card reads as Talero and not as MFFU gold, but the shared .mod class gives every module a 2px gold left edge, Talero included. The design rule is working against the component decision.

Fix: border-left-color: rgba(79,163,255,.55) on this card only. It still reads as a new module, in the right company's colour.

Medium

6. "Coming soon" tells people there is nothing to do

The waitlist is open right now. "Coming soon" describes the brokerage accurately and the action inaccurately, and it is the reason to scroll past.

Fix: Waitlist open. Equally true, and it is the difference between an announcement and an invitation.

Medium

7. Two filled buttons in one row

Refer a friend ships a filled Copy link button and Talero ships a filled blue CTA, side by side. The design rules say one action per view and reserve fills for the most useful thing on screen. For most traders that is the referral link, not a brokerage that has not opened.

Fix: Talero takes .opp-cta.neutral until the brokerage actually opens, then earns the fill.

Medium

8. You cannot tell whether the card works

Only a click event was specced. With no impression event there is no denominator, so no click through rate, and no way to compare this surface against the header tab or the homepage rail.

Fix: add talero_card_view on render, carrying the suppression state, so suppressed impressions are counted rather than silently missing.

Medium

9. "Talero by MFB Capital" implies separateness without saying it

A trader reading a card inside their MFFU dashboard will reasonably assume it is an MFFU product with a different name. The regulatory line names the entity but never says it is a different company.

Fix: three words. "Talero by MFB Capital, a separate company". Costs nothing, and it is the sentence a regulator would look for.

Decided

10. The CTA no longer drops a trader out of their session

The spec said the CTA opens talero.com but not how. A same tab navigation pulls a funded trader out of a live dashboard, possibly with positions open.

Done: target="_blank" rel="noopener", with the aria-label saying it opens in a new tab.

Not compliance approved. Every Talero surface is introducing broker promotional material and needs pre-review, versioning and archiving. The card above carries the handoff copy unchanged apart from the link behaviour, so it can be reviewed as specced.
