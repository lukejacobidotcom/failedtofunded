Behaviour spec

Component
Add one tab to ProductSwitcherBar. No new component. The markup mirrors the existing tabs exactly: an <a class="tab"> holding an icon, then labels with title and subtitle, and the badge nested inside the title span.
Icon
Needs a real asset. Futures ships futures-icon.ac5bdd1e869c.svg at 20×24 and Perps ships perps-icon.f5f8c4664e9b.png at 24×24. Add talero-icon.svg under /images/mffu/static/. The blue square standing in for it here is a placeholder, not artwork.
Label
The word is Talero with a Waitlist badge. Never "Brokerage", never "Open an account". A waitlist is a notification list; an account is a solicitation. That one word decides which compliance category the tab sits in.
Badge
Same badge component, recoloured: background #0048FF with white text. Perps green #34C759 with #02040E text is reserved for live products, and the dark text fails contrast on blue anyway. The word is WAITLIST, never NEW.
Destination
https://www.talero.com/waitlist?partner=MFFU, matching the ?partner=MFFU convention the Perps tab already uses rather than introducing a UTM string. Unlike the other two tabs it is target="_blank" rel="noopener", because it leaves the estate.
Subtitle
Futures brokerage, coming soon, tinted rgb(143,180,255). This mirrors how the Perps tab tints its subtitle rgb(127,199,154) to carry its own brand colour.
Separation
A 1px vertical rule before the tab, plus Talero blue on the icon, badge and subtitle. Futures and Perps are My Funded Futures. Talero is MFB Capital, LLC. The bar must not imply one firm.
Disclosure
Hover and focus reveal MFB Capital, LLC · a separate company under the subtitle, 200ms ease-out, disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. The full CFTC and NFA line lives on the destination page.
Active state
Not needed yet: the tab always navigates away, so it never carries aria-current. If Talero ever gets pages inside the estate, follow the existing pattern and give it a taleroActive class whose inset box shadow is #0048FF, matching how Futures uses #3A82F7 and Perps uses #34C759.
Focus
Unchanged from the component: outline: rgb(52,199,89) solid 2px; outline-offset: -3px. Green is the bar's focus colour for every tab and Talero should not invent its own.
Analytics
data-cta="header-talero". Report clicks against waitlist signups so we can tell whether the bar is doing the work.
Sequencing
This is the highest reach surface we own: every page, every visitor, logged out included. Ship it after talero.com carries commissions, the FCM name and its how it works ladder above the fold. Opening this tap into the page as it stands today wastes the best impression we get.
Not compliance approved. Every Talero surface is introducing broker promotional material and needs pre-review, versioning and archiving before it ships.
